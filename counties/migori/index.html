Migori County internet and electricity coverage

Migori ranks 30th of 47 counties on my composite connectivity score, at 42.2 out of 100. That puts it in the Low tier.

30.4% of people in Migori use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 23rd place nationally. Electricity access is 28.9% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 33rd.

Average mobile download speed is 29.2 Mbps, 30th of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 59.3 per 100 km², which ranks 20th.
