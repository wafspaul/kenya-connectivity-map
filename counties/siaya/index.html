Siaya County: internet and electricity coverage

Siaya ranks 24th of 47 counties on my composite connectivity score, at 47.1 out of 100. That puts it in the Medium tier.

27.4% of people in Siaya use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 30th place nationally. Electricity access is 30.4% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 32nd.

Average mobile download speed is 23.5 Mbps, 40th of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 114.4 per 100 km², which ranks 11th.
