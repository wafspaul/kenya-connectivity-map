37.0% of people in Kajiado use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits above it, in 11th place nationally. Electricity access is 57.8% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 9th.

Average mobile download speed is 44.9 Mbps, 5th of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 15.6 per 100 km², which ranks 32nd.
