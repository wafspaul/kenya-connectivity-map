Nandi County: internet and electricity coverage

Nandi ranks 27th of 47 counties on my composite connectivity score, at 45.7 out of 100. That puts it in the Medium tier.

24.4% of people in Nandi use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 36th place nationally. Electricity access is 41.2% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 23rd.

Average mobile download speed is 34.2 Mbps, 21st of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 39.8 per 100 km², which ranks 25th.
