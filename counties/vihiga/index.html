18.3% of people in Vihiga use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 42nd place nationally. Electricity access is 45.6% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 17th.

Average mobile download speed is 26.3 Mbps, 37th of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 164.6 per 100 km², which ranks 6th.
