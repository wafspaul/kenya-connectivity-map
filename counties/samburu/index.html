18.9% of people in Samburu use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 41st place nationally. Electricity access is 16.1% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 43rd.

I flag the speed figure for Samburu. A handful of Starlink users can pull a county average up, so I leave Samburu out of the speed ranking and read its tower density instead.
