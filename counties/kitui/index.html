Kitui County: internet and electricity coverage

Kitui ranks 39th of 47 counties on my composite connectivity score, at 28.9 out of 100. That puts it in the Low tier.

26.2% of people in Kitui use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 33rd place nationally. Electricity access is 23.8% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 39th.

Average mobile download speed is 54.1 Mbps, 2nd of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 7.0 per 100 km², which ranks 37th.
