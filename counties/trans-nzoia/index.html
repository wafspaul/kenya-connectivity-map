Trans Nzoia County internet and electricity coverage

Trans Nzoia ranks 28th of 47 counties on my composite connectivity score, at 45.4 out of 100. That puts it in the Medium tier.

30.6% of people in Trans Nzoia use the internet. The national figure on this map is 35%, so the county sits below it, in 21st place nationally. Electricity access is 38.9% against a national 38.7%, which ranks 24th.

Average mobile download speed is 37.7 Mbps, 17th of the 46 counties with an unflagged speed figure. Tower density is 75.5 per 100 km², which ranks 18th.
